About me

I am currently a master’s student in Artificial Intelligence at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Prof. Juepeng Zheng. I received my bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from Fujian Normal University in 2024.

In 2027, I will begin graduate studies at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School, Tsinghua University, where I plan to focus on hyperspectral remote sensing and foundation models for remote sensing, under the research guidance of Prof. Lizhe Wang and Dr. Ruyi Feng.

During my master’s studies, I have worked on agricultural remote sensing, particularly fine-grained parcel and boundary delineation, cross-regional generalization, and multimodal understanding of complex agricultural landscapes. My broader research goal is to develop data-efficient and generalizable AI methods for interpreting Earth observation data.

I welcome discussions and collaborations on hyperspectral imaging, remote sensing foundation models, and geospatial AI. Feel free to get in touch.

Research interests

Upcoming research focus · From 2027

  • 01
    Hyperspectral remote sensing

    Spectral–spatial representation learning and hyperspectral scene understanding, with an emphasis on generalization across sensors, domains, and applications.

  • 02
    Foundation models for remote sensing

    Exploring pretrained and multimodal models that can reason over spectral information and adapt to diverse Earth observation tasks.

Master’s research · 2024–2027

  • 03
    Fine-grained agricultural mapping

    Understanding individual parcels and their boundaries in high-resolution imagery, especially in complex terraced landscapes.

  • 04
    Learning across geographic regions

    Adapting remote sensing models to new regions while preserving boundary geometry and parcel structure.

  • 05
    Multimodal agricultural intelligence

    Combining imagery, language, and terrain information for agricultural scene understanding.
